### 7.2 Color Grade Recipe

**Talking head (studio footage):**
1. Apply no LUT. Work in natural / flat grade.
2. Set white balance to neutral-cool (5,500–6,000 K). Do not push warm.
3. Saturation: pull down to ~70–75% of native saturation.
4. Shadows: leave natural (no lift, no crush). YLOW should land around 20–30.
5. Highlights: gentle S-curve rolloff. Protect the white clothing.
6. Optionally: slight teal push in the shadows via Color Wheels (aligns skin tone to the teal-heavy background without fighting it).

**Motion graphics:**
- Background: `#0d2415` to `#122018` (very dark forest green — not pure black).
- Accent green: `#3ab83a` (neon green, RGB 58/184/58).
- All other surfaces: silver/chrome material or white with glow.
- Avoid warm colors except for intentional accent (red for negative/loss data).

### 7.4 Kinetic Text Rules

1. Sync each word or short phrase to the moment it is **spoken** in dialogue.
2. Animate in with a **fast scale pop** (0.8 → 1.0 scale over 3–5 frames, optional overshoot to 1.05 with ease-out bounce).
3. Hold the word on screen until the next word appears or a cut occurs.
4. Do **not** stack more than 3–4 words on screen simultaneously.
5. Center-align all text horizontally.
6. Position text between the subject's chest and mid-torso (avoid covering face or hands).
7. Always apply a dark drop shadow (opacity ~60%, distance 4–6 px, softness 8 px) for legibility over white/light clothing.

---

### 7.5 Cut Rhythm Rules

1. **Pacing target:** Medium — 3–6 s average shot duration.
2. **Hard cuts only** — no dissolves, no wipes, no fades between content segments.
3. **Cut placement:** At natural dialogue sentence-breaks. Never mid-word.
4. **MG insert rule:** Cut to motion graphics at the moment the speaker *introduces a new concept*. Cut back to speaker after the concept is visualized (typically 2–6 s).
5. **Hook:** The very first segment (0–2 s) should be a live talking head. MG can follow immediately after the opening hook statement.
6. **Conclusion stretch:** Allow a longer talking head hold (8–12 s) at the end for the CTA delivery.

---

### 7.6 Motion Graphic Structure

Each MG segment should follow this layer order:

```
1. Background: dark forest green (#0d2415) solid or subtle radial gradient
2. Particle layer: scattered glowing white dots, slow float animation
3. Crosshair decorations: small + marks at corners or accent positions
4. Primary 3D asset (brain / coin / chart card) — centered or offset
5. Text layer (headline + pill buttons)
6. Brand X mark overlaid on relevant elements
```

Animation timing for list reveals:
- Stagger each pill-button in by 8–12 frames after the previous one.
- Each pill animates in from left (slides in from -20% X offset) + fades from 0→1 opacity over 8 frames.
- Diamond bullet point appears 4 frames before each pill.

### 7.9 Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Cut to MG exactly when a new point is introduced verbally.
- Keep kinetic text synchronized word-by-word with dialogue.
- Use the neon green consistently across both live and MG segments.
- Maintain a single MCU locked shot for all talking head footage.
- Use hard cuts exclusively.
- Keep MG segments 2–6 s. Never let them run longer than the point they're illustrating.

**Don't:**
- Add transition effects (dissolves, wipes) — they conflict with the hard-cut energy.
- Vary the shot size in the talking head (no push-ins, no B-roll over the speaker).
- Use more than 2 font weights in any single segment.
- Apply a cinematic warm LUT — the look is intentionally cool-neutral.
- Let kinetic text overlap the subject's face.
- Use full saturated color fills — the palette is intentionally dark and desaturated except for the brand green.
